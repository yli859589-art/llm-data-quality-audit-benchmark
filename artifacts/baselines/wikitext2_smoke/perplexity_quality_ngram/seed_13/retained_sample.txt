The history of language modeling includes statistical models, neural networks, and transformer architectures. A benchmark corpus should include coherent passages that allow a small model to learn short-range dependencies.

Small language model experiments are useful when they are reproducible. Fixed token budgets, deterministic seeds, and generated artifacts make it possible to compare interventions without changing the training surface.

Dataset cards record source, license notes, fallback status, document counts, retained characters, random seeds, runtime, and command lines. These fields make a compact repository easier to audit.
