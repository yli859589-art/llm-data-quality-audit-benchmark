# Level 3 Claim Boundary

This document defines public wording for the Level 3 route.

## Allowed Current Claims

- The repository implements a reproducible audit pipeline.
- Step 0 through Step 10A infrastructure exists.
- Step 10A freezes the Level 3 heavy protocol and preflight checks.
- URD-Selector is implemented as a smoke-verified selector pipeline.
- Evaluation and mechanism-analysis frameworks exist.
- Historical evidence shows HDQS++ v3 did not outperform raw under the current
  historical small-setting evidence.
- Current readiness after Step 10A is at most `LEVEL3_PIPELINE_READY`.

## Disallowed Current Claims

- forbidden claim: URD beats raw.
- forbidden claim: URD improves PPL.
- forbidden claim: URD improves downstream.
- forbidden claim: URD is SOTA.
- forbidden claim: C4/Gopher/CCNet official reproduction completed.
- forbidden claim: medium/large-lite evidence completed.
- forbidden claim: 500M-token or 1B-token data completed.
- forbidden claim: Level 3 completed.
- forbidden claim: CCF-B ready.
- forbidden claim: CCF-A ready.
- forbidden claim: full-scale mechanism conclusion proved.
- forbidden claim: downstream official benchmarks completed.
- forbidden claim: Step 10B heavy execution completed.

## Future-Only Claims

The following claims are future-only and require Step 10B/10C evidence:

- Level 3 completed artifact.
- Strong CCF-B-style project prototype.
- Weak CCF-A-style research flavor.
- Full-scale URD effectiveness evidence.
- Full baseline matrix evidence.
- Full downstream evidence.
- Full mechanism evidence.
